# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People discovering Zaineb's software engineering work, including recruiters, engineering teams, potential clients, collaborators, and other developers.

## Product Purpose

Present Zaineb Messaoudi's projects and engineering practice in an engaging way that helps visitors understand her work and contact her. Make it clear that she is seeking a six-month PFE internship starting in January 2027.

## Positioning

The portfolio is an interactive third-person game-world journey where a traveler follows a 3D road map through Zaineb's complete original portfolio and recent verified GitHub projects, rather than a traditional CV.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive to browse projects, understand Zaineb's experience, or assess her fit for collaboration or a PFE internship. The portfolio is bilingual in English and French and links to the public GitHub account `Zaineb-Messaoudi`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site presents Zaineb's software engineering, full-stack, and applied AI work.
- The project showcase should reflect recent, relevant repositories from her GitHub account.
- Preserve the full original portfolio content, including About, professional summary, four internships, education, detailed skills, certifications, contact, and existing project case studies.
- The main journey uses a third-person character traveling along a three-dimensional road map; interactive stops lead to the portfolio sections.
- Project media areas must support visitor-provided images or videos; keep accurate text and repository links usable before media is supplied.
- Clearly communicate PFE availability from January 2027 without making the whole experience read like a CV.
- Preserve working project links and contact options.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Zaineb Messaoudi.
- Keep the experience interactive and game-inspired rather than CV-shaped.
- The selected experience is a third-person character traveling a 3D game-world road map, not a flat or retro console.
- Be clear and accurate about the January 2027 PFE internship search.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing portfolio content and assets under `src/` and `public/`.
- Public repositories at `https://github.com/Zaineb-Messaoudi`, including the recent Learnwise and LegalGraph (`legal-semantic-app`) projects.
- Existing project details include Oralis, TalentLens, and a cross-platform maternity-tracking project.

## Product Principles

- Let visitors explore real work, not just scan a list of credentials.
- Keep the project descriptions grounded in the repositories and documented contributions.
- Make the PFE opportunity easy to understand without reducing the portfolio to a job application.
- Keep useful contact and source links close to the work.
